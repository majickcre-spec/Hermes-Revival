Notes & attribution

A memory practice for the Heads of Things (Book I of the Divine Pymander), in Majick's Modernization. Original wording is John Everard's 1650 translation.

The 59 finalized (locked) heads are included. Parked, flagged and not-yet-worked verses (37–39, 43, 61–62, 69, 80–88) are left out until they're settled.
Mixed Path shuffles every question type and leans on the heads missed most — the fullest way to carry them.
Recall is active recall with honest self-rating; the strongest way to commit them to memory.
Focused practice drills one style at a time: Choose, Fill, Match, Build.
Stars ⭐ mark heads mastered. Hearts ♥ are earned for returning and trying again. Beans 🫘 are earned from stars and spent in the shop. Progress saves on this device.
