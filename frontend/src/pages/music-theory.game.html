Majick Music University

Three ways to learn, Star.

Music lives in the head, the ear, and the hands. So we learn it three ways: study the ideas, train your ear with our own games, and play it on the keyboard and instruments — all right here.

🎯 Know it👂 Hear it🥁 Feel it🎹 Play it
Know it — study & memorize
The ideas behind the music — intervals, keys, the circle of fifths, and how scales & modes are built. Flip-cards, quizzes, and interactive builders.
Hear it — train your ear right here
Ear-training games — hear a note, chord, interval or scale and learn to name it by sound.
Feel it — rhythm & breath
Find your timing — watch a groove, tap it back, and play rhythm on the djembe. Settles the body, too.
Play an instrument
Guitar, bass, ukulele, piano, drums and handpan — tuners, fretboards, chords, scales, strumming and play-alongs.
Make your own music
Write, record, make beats, sample, arrange and mix — build a whole song and export it (audio + MIDI) to your DAW.
Measure it — brain & heart beta
Needs a Muse headset and Chrome/Edge on a computer or Android (Web Bluetooth). Streams live brainwaves + heart rhythm straight to the page.
Play it — at the keyboard
Open the 🎹 Piano above, or sit at your real keyboard. Do each one, then tap “Did it.”
