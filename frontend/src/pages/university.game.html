Why this exists

A music school, for everybody

I went to Musicians Institute in Hollywood. It changed how I play and how I hear. But most people never get that — the tuition, the city, the timing. And even for those who do, the hardest part is after: staying in motion once the campus is behind you.

So I built this. To help musicians keep progressing — after school, between schools, or for anyone who never got to go. So that learning music, producing music, and playing an instrument is available to everybody. And to understand the connection between music, meditation, and the evolution of consciousness.

— Travis MajickFounder · Musicians Institute graduate

The method

The MI way, with no campus

MI’s whole philosophy is learn by doing: you play from day one, you perform every week, you learn from working musicians, and you graduate ready to work. This carries that method to your pocket — and adds the one thing a campus can’t.

01

Play from day one

Real instruments in your hands — piano, guitar, bass, ukulele, handpan, djembe. You make sound first and learn the theory as you need it, the way MI teaches on the stand.

02

Feedback from the craft

Drop in a track and The Instructor grades it like a midterm — gain staging, balance, low end, dynamics, stereo — genre by genre, and tells you what a pro would fix.

03

Progress you can see

Every version of every track is saved and scored, so you watch what progressed and what regressed. You always know you’re moving forward.

04

Learn from the music

Ear training, chords and real songs you build yourself, beats you sequence by hand. You learn by making the thing, not reading about it.

The curriculum

Your departments

Instruments
Piano, guitar, bass, ukulele, handpan, djembe — playable, with finger charts and patterns.
Ear Training
Chords, scales and intervals by ear — the skill every pro is built on.
Beat Maker
Sequence layered beats and motifs by hand, loop them, export MIDI.
The Studio
Multi-track recorder — stack your parts into sections, then mix them.
Learn a Song
Bring in a song, get a play-along chart with loop, tempo and transpose.
The Instructor
Genre-specific mix critique, graded, with your progress tracked over time.
Schumann Resonance
The Earth’s frequency as meditation, binaural, or a full song — the resonance wing.
Song Project
Assemble your sections into a finished song — your final.
